💧 Waterfall Strategy — T9 & T10 Troop Promotion

How to double (or triple) your VS points from the same troops on Friday

📢 Introduction

Hello everyone,

This guide is especially important for every member who has already unlocked T9 troops, and even more valuable for those who have unlocked T10.

We want to explain a simple but very effective way to organize your troop training during the week and earn many more VS points on Friday – VS Day 5: Total Mobilization using the same troops.

💡 The Main Idea

If you have T9 or T10 troops, don't spend the entire week training ready-made T9/T10 troops directly.

Instead, during the week, train and accumulate T7 troops and keep them in your Drill Ground.

Then wait until Friday – VS Day 5 and promote them step by step:

T7 → T8 +points ✅
T8 → T9 +points ✅
T9 → T10 +points ✅ (if you have T10)
This allows you to earn VS points twice (or three times if you have T10) from the same group of troops. 🔥
📊 Simple Example: 5,000 Troops

We are using 5,000 troops only as a simple example. You can apply the same strategy with 10,000, 20,000, or more troops depending on how many you accumulate during the week.

5,000 T7 → T8≈ 1,057,500 pts
5,000 T8 → T9≈ 1,175,000 pts
5,000 T9 → T10≈ 1,292,500 pts
TOTAL (with T10)3,525,000 pts 🔥
Just 5,000 troops can generate more than 3.5M VS points when the full T7→T8→T9→T10 promotion chain is used correctly. The more troops you save during the week, the greater the benefit on Friday.
⚠️ The point values shown are approximate and may vary slightly depending on your Alliance Duel research that boosts VS points.
⚠️ VERY IMPORTANT — Barracks Levels

For this strategy to work correctly, you need to have Barracks at different levels — each level unlocks a certain promotion ceiling.

Do not upgrade all your Barracks to the same level, because:

A Level 27 Barracks can only upgrade troops to T9. If you select T7 troops to upgrade on a Lv.27 Barracks, they will be promoted directly to T9 — skipping T8 entirely, and you lose those points. The same logic applies below Lv.30: it cannot upgrade troops to T10.

This is why you need a Barracks between Lv.24 and Lv.26 for the T7 → T8 step, a Lv.27 Barracks for the T8 → T9 step, and a Lv.30 Barracks for the T9 → T10 step.

Barracks LevelRolePromotion
Lv.24 – Lv.26First stepT7 → T8
Lv.27Second stepT8 → T9
Lv.30Third stepT9 → T10
For production, any Barracks that has already unlocked T7 can produce T7 troops, even if its level is higher. For upgrades, however, the Barracks level matters a lot — it determines the highest Tier that Barracks can promote troops to.

Here is a real example of how to organize your Barracks for this strategy (assuming you have a Lv.30 Barracks if you've unlocked T10):

BarracksUsed For
All BarracksProduce T7 troops all week (any level that can produce T7 works)
1 Barracks Lv.24–26Upgrade T7 → T8 on Friday
1 Barracks Lv.27Upgrade T8 → T9 on Friday
1 Barracks Lv.30Upgrade T9 → T10 on Friday (if you have T10)
Key point: any Barracks that has unlocked T7 can produce T7 troops, even at a higher level. But for upgrades, the level matters — it determines the highest Tier it can promote to. Keep at least one Barracks dedicated to each step of the chain (Lv.24–26, Lv.27, and Lv.30) so you can perform each promotion stage separately.
📅 What Should You Do During the Week?
  • ✅ Train T7 troops all week on all your Barracks.
  • ✅ Accumulate as many T7 as possible in your Drill Ground.
  • ⛔ Do not rush to promote them to T8, T9, or T10 during the week.
  • ✅ Save them for Friday – Total Mobilization.
  • ✅ Use your Lv.24–26 Barracks to promote T7 → T8.
  • ✅ Then use your Lv.27 Barracks to promote T8 → T9.
  • ✅ If you've unlocked T10, use your Lv.30 Barracks to promote T9 → T10.
This way, the same troops generate points through 2 or 3 separate promotion stages. 💪
🎯 Why Is This Strategy Better?

The goal is not simply to train as many troops as possible. The goal is to extract as many VS points as possible from the troops you already have.

Instead of doing one direct promotion:

T7 → T10 Points × 1 ❌

We split it on Friday into 3 consecutive steps:

T7 → T8 → T9 → T10 Points × 3 ✅
This allows us to score from every promotion stage along the way — not just the final result. 🔥
🟣 A Note on T10

The Barracks level required to produce/promote T10 troops is Lv.30.

The points earned in the T9 → T10 step depend mainly on the Tier the troop reaches (≈258 pts/troop), and may vary slightly depending on your Alliance Duel research that boosts VS points.

📋 Summary
  • ✅ During the week → Train and accumulate T7 troops.
  • ✅ On Friday → T7 → T8 → T9 → T10 (whatever you've unlocked, as separate steps).
  • ✅ Keep Barracks at different levels: Lv.24–26 for T7→T8, Lv.27 for T8→T9, and Lv.30 for T9→T10.
  • ⛔ Do not waste promotion opportunities before Friday.
Remember, 5,000 troops is only a simple example. Apply the same principle to as many troops as you can save. The more T7 troops you accumulate during the week, the more opportunities you have to generate huge VS points on Friday — especially if you have T10 and use the full chain.
Don't waste your promotion opportunities before the correct VS day. Save your troops, organize your Barracks, and make every promotion count. 💪🔥
