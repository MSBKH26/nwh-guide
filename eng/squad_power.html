🔍 Diagnosing squad weakness — where's the problem?
🛡️ Case 1 — The frontline dies fast
Direct causes: weak Armor/Radar · low Stars · weak defensive Skills
General factors that drag performance down: low Troop Tier · a Type Center behind schedule — these lower overall power, but they're not the direct reason Murphy dies before Kimberly.
💡
Don't add Damage yet. Fix the frontline's survivability first — DPS doesn't matter if the Front is collapsing.
⚔️ Case 2 — The Front holds but Damage is weak
Signs: the fight drags on · the enemy stays alive · Kimberly / Stetmann aren't landing enough Damage
CheckCannon · Data Chip · Hero Skills · Hero Stars · Exclusive Weapon · Offensive Tech
💡
Your next investment should go into DPS, not more defense.
💥 Case 3 — The backline dies even though the Front is alive
Causes: AoE reaching the backline · weak Radar/Armor on your DPS · a Tech gap · Counter
💡
Don't just keep raising Cannon and Chip. Start improving Backline survivability.

📊 What should I watch in the Battle Report?
DPS dies first → Start by checking: Targeting + Damage taken + Armor/Radar on the Backline + current Gear distribution
Tank dies first → Start by checking: Targeting + Damage taken + Defense Gear + Stars + Tech on the Front
Everyone survives but Damage is weak → A DPS problem — Cannon / Skills / Weapon / Tech
You always lose to the same squad type → Check Counter first; if it keeps happening, plan a second squad to answer it
Lower-Power opponent, but stronger troops → Start by checking: Troop Tier / Tech
⚠️
Before any investment: compare matching heroes on the enemy side, Damage dealt vs. taken, and Skill level. One battle may not be enough to diagnose — look for a pattern across several fights.

🔷 Shards — within Squad 1, who gets them first?
🎯
Don't split shards evenly on autopilot. Level up the hero who removes the biggest bottleneck in the squad.
Main DPS High priority — raising their stars directly and clearly raises Damage
Frontline If they die fast and that stops your DPS from working → priority
Support Not to be ignored, but priority depends on the stage and their actual role

🎖️ Skill Medals — choose by role
DPS Priority to skills that directly affect Damage
Tank / Defense Priority to skills that boost survival or reduce Damage taken
Support Priority based on the value of the buff they give the squad
❌ Don't Spread Skill Medals evenly across every hero
💡
Don't raise every skill equally. Pick the skill that gives the best combat result for that hero's current role.
🔎 Where's your account's bottleneck?
🎯
Don't always look for the strongest upgrade. Look for the weakest system holding the squad back.
Great stars + weak Gear → Gear is the bottleneck
Great Gear + weak Skills → Skills are the bottleneck
Great heroes + only T7 → Troops are the bottleneck
Everything's good but T10 is far off → Special Forces / Research is the bottleneck
Everything's balanced but Drone is behind → Drone is your long-term bottleneck

🏗️ Buildings — ask yourself "why upgrade this now?"
🏛️ HQ Raise it if the next level unlocks: Hero Level / Barracks / a Building / Research
🚗 Tank Center Direct buffs to the Tank Squad — raise it according to your squad's priority
✈️/🚀 Other Centers Don't raise the Aircraft/Missile Center at the same pace if you're not using them yet
🏠 Barracks Raise it when you need a higher Troop Tier or the Waterfall strategy
🔬 Tech Center Raise it when your current Research is being capped by its level
💡
Don't upgrade a building because it's "important" — upgrade it because the next level serves a clear goal.
🤖 Drone
The four Drone systems
1 — Drone Level Combat Data (raises the level) + Drone Parts (needed for some upgrades) — the level strengthens every other system
2 — Components Progress through their own system (Component chests) — give the squad extra buffs
3 — Skill Chips Add specialized combat effects — some are aimed at specific squad types
4 — Combat Boost Cumulative rewards for the whole squad — unlocked at milestone points
🏆
Combat Boost milestones:
Lv 150 → total +1 Skill Chip Boost  ·  Lv 300 → total +2  ·  Lv 450 → total +3
These are cumulative totals at each milestone, not additions that stack on top of each other — plan toward the next milestone.
When to spend and when to save?
VS Day coming up Best to save resources that count toward VS points
Drone way behind Don't postpone all growth — develop it gradually from the moment it unlocks
📈
Develop the Drone steadily from the moment it unlocks. Its buffs cover the whole squad — a long delay is hard to make up for later. A big investment should be judged against the next milestone and your available resources, not an absolute rule.

🏆 Golden rules — remember these
1️⃣
Don't spread your resources across 3 squads
Two strong squads beat three incomplete ones.
2️⃣
As a rule: shards → Squad 1 until 5×5
5 heroes × 5 stars — that's the general cutoff line. If the next upgrade on Squad 1 gives limited benefit compared to a core upgrade on Squad 2, re-evaluate by actual benefit.
3️⃣
Gear develops by what shows up in combat — not by hero name
Open the Battle Report first — who dies first gives you a lead to check, not a final verdict. Check Damage taken, Skills, and Targeting before any investment.
4️⃣
Don't look for the strongest upgrade — look for the weakest bottleneck
The biggest combat improvement comes from fixing your weakest point, not strengthening what's already strong.
5️⃣
Tech and Special Forces — start them early if T10 is your goal
Special Forces is the only road to T10 — delaying it costs a lot.
6️⃣
Drone isn't "the endgame" — develop it gradually
On VS days, hold resources that score points when spent — Drone resources are one of them. Balance daily power against building up VS points.
7️⃣
Gear and Shards switch to Squad 2 on different timelines
Shards move over after 5×5 — but Gear can stay the first squad's priority for longer.
8️⃣
Real power is combat performance, not the number alone
Focusing on fixing the bottleneck gives a better combat result than spreading resources thin.
