⚔️ What is the Alliance VS Battle?

The Alliance VS Battle is a 6-day event where your alliance competes against another in a variety of daily challenges. Each day focuses on a different theme, and every member earns points by completing daily tasks. The alliance that scores the most points wins the day, and whoever wins the most days wins the week. You'll earn daily and weekly rewards based on both your individual performance and your alliance's performance.

🔑 Key to success: winning isn't only about power — it's about smart planning, good timing, and coordination between alliance members. Each day focuses on a different aspect of your base and progress, so know what to do and when to do it.

🏅 Alliance VS Rewards
🥇 Daily Ranking Rewards
If you rank among the top 30 players in daily individual points, you'll receive special bonus rewards.
📦 Daily Tier Rewards
Every day you participate in VS you get a reward package regardless of winning or losing — all you need is to complete the daily tasks. At the end of the week, your alliance also earns extra rewards based on the VS result.
🏆 League Settlement Rewards
By the end of the VS league, your alliance must accumulate at least 6,000,000 points. The rewards you receive depend heavily on your alliance's tier (Silver, Gold, or Diamond) in the event.
🎯 Conclusion

The Alliance VS Battle isn't just about who's the strongest — it's about who plans smarter, times things better, and works with their team as one cohesive unit. Each day focuses on a different aspect of your base and progress. Know what to do, when to do it, and how to get the most out of every resource.


Sync your actions with the Arms Race, save items like resources, Construction/Research/Training Speedups and Honor Badges, and focus on the upgrades that matter most. Planning for the Alliance VS rewards is just as important as participating itself — plan, coordinate, and finish the week strong! 🏆

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
When should I open UR fragments?
➡️ On Hero day (Train Heroes) — this is the best day to open and use UR hero fragments.
When should I use speedups?
➡️ Depending on their type, on the matching day: Construction Speedups on Base day, Research Speedups on Science day, Training Speedups on Hero day.
Should I save my Radar Missions?
➡️ Yes, save them ahead of Radar days (Monday, Wednesday, Friday) to get the maximum possible points.
