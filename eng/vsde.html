📦 Weekly Chest-Opening Plan

Last War: Survival — How to reach 3.6M and 7.2M every VS day

📖 How to Use This Guide
Pick the VS day from the tabs below → follow the example → open at least 3.6M → if you have extra resources, push on to 7.2M.
3.6M = Minimum 7.2M = All Chests
🏆 Golden Rules for VS
  • Don't spend a resource before its assigned day.
  • Save your Radar Missions for the next day whenever the guide tells you to.
  • 3.6M is the minimum, not the final goal.
  • If you can reach 7.2M, don't stop at 3.6M.
  • The numbers in the examples can be adjusted to match your own resources.
