❄️ 1. Season 2 Introduction

Season 2 introduces a brand-new system built around Temperature. Your base has its own temperature, and the map around you has an ambient temperature that drops the closer you get to the center (down to -80°C at the Capital). Your base's temperature gradually drifts toward the ambient temperature surrounding it.

If your base stays below -20°C for more than 5 minutes, it freezes — transfers and Rallies are then blocked, with only random teleports and alliance transfers remaining available.

To counter this, you have:

  • 🔥 Personal High-Heat Furnace — raises your heat and virus resistance, consuming more coal in Overdrive mode.
  • 🏛️ Alliance Furnace — built on Level-1 land, warms bases within its range, and needs coal from members.
  • 🏭 Titanium Alloy Factories — produce the titanium alloy needed to develop the personal furnace and seasonal buildings.
  • ⛏️ Dig Sites — your ongoing coal source and your first owned territory.

These systems are all connected: coal from dig sites is used to build and upgrade furnaces and factories, which in turn protect you from freezing and unlock the virus resistance needed to fight the polar monsters and take more territory.

🧱 3. Base Placement Layout — NWH Strategy
Internal NWH strategy — not an official in-game rule

Each base occupies 3×3 tiles (9 tiles). NWH's adopted layout leaves exactly two empty tiles between the edges of every pair of adjacent bases, both horizontally and vertically: 3 occupied tiles + 2 empty tiles + 3 occupied tiles.

Goals: expand NWH's area of influence, keep movement organized, reduce open spots for the enemy, and prepare a protected rear zone for the Alliance Furnace.

🌡️ 4. Temperature and Storms

The temperature range runs from -270°C to +300°C. Your base's temperature gradually drifts toward the surrounding ambient temperature.

  • ⏳ Freeze warning: staying below -20°C starts a 5-minute countdown.
  • 🧊 Full freeze: after 5 continuous minutes below -20°C — transfers and rallies are blocked (random teleports and alliance transfers remain available).
  • 🔥 Thaw: staying above -20°C for 5 minutes removes the freeze. Dropping below -20°C again, even for a moment, resets the countdown.
  • 🧨 Breaking the ice: allies can break the ice manually (5 strikes, 5 energy points per strike).
  • ✈️ Recon Plane: adds roughly +2°C per strike, but its effect is temporary and fades quickly.
💡 Use the "Warm Up" tab to see which allies' heat has dropped dangerously fast, instead of checking every member manually. You can also enable an auto-setting to run your personal furnace automatically at the start of a Blizzard, to avoid freezing if you're away from the game the moment it hits.
Note: base temperature doesn't only affect freeze risk — it also affects stats and extra buffs/debuffs for the base. The source doesn't give exact percentages for this effect, so we don't quote any specific figure here.

⛏️ 5. Coal and Resources

Coal sources: Dig Sites (ongoing production), the Season Pass and Weekly Pass, scattered Supply Collections (only appear when the land's temperature reaches 0°C), season missions, Goals, radar missions, zombie-kill rewards, Hero's Return tickets, and your first occupation of a city or dig site. (The Polar Dishes/Snowflake-feeding event adds an extra coal source, but it starts from Week 2 — outside the scope of this guide.)

Uses: building and upgrading seasonal buildings, running the personal furnace, donating to the Alliance Furnace.

💡 Tip: use the "Claim all hourly" button in the Dig Sites tab so you don't miss cumulative coal production.
💡 Tip: balance personal needs (your own furnace) against collective needs (the Alliance Furnace) — don't empty your personal stockpile into the alliance before securing your own heat, and vice versa when a strong storm is approaching.

🏭 6. Titanium Alloy Factories

Production starts at 720/hour at Level 1 and reaches 21,600/hour at Level 30 — each level adds a flat 720/hour.

A new factory only unlocks once the previous factory reaches Level 15. The Weekly Pass grants an extra factory immediately, and this factory keeps producing only for the duration of the pass — it's a paid, optional feature that ends when the pass expires and isn't a permanent gain, nor is it required to complete Week 1 progress.

🏛️ 7. Alliance Furnace

Built on Level-1 land. Starting and managing the repair process (via: Alliance → Alliance Furnace → Coordinates) is restricted to R4/R5 only. Actual participation in the repair is done by any member sending their squads to reinforce the furnace — the same mechanic as stronghold repairs in Season 1 — where up to 20 reinforcements can take part (not a mandatory number to complete the process). It only warms bases located within its range — so members need to gather near it.

NWH strategy

Members with low personal heat or a less-developed personal furnace can be placed closer to the Alliance Furnace to benefit from its warming range, within the bounds of that range and in line with NWH's general placement decisions (Section 3).

🎉 8. Week 1 Events

  • 🎯 Mission Goal: a specific individual achievement (such as running the furnace for 600 seconds) — may only unlock after the collective chest goal is met (a 12-hour gap in some cases).

🗺️ 9. Dig Sites and Cities

The first land to secure is a Level-1 Dig Site. Expansion afterward follows an adjacency principle (including diagonal connections from corners).

  • 📊 Maximum: 4 dig sites per alliance, regardless of how many cities you own.
  • 📅 Daily cap: only two occupations per day. Once reached, neutral or owned sites can't be attacked.
  • ⏱️ Occupation window: the alliance must reach 100% within 4 hours, or the attempt fails.
  • 🛡️ Post-occupation protection: 36 hours, guarded by site guards.
  • 🦣 Requirements: the monsters must not be frozen (thawing a frozen monster first requires sending a squad), and several rallies are needed since each strike only deals a percentage of damage.
  • 🧱 Reinforcement: up to 10 squads can be sent to reinforce a dig site after the monster is defeated.
  • 🚪 Abandoning: takes one hour; the protection period must end before any other alliance can re-occupy it.
🎁 First-participation rewards (any one of the following is enough to earn it): joining a regular Rally against a Mutant Beast with a strong squad, starting/joining a "participation rally" with just one weak hero squad, or reinforcing a dig site during its occupation even for a few seconds.
⚠️ These are the occupation and participation rules as given in the strategy guide — deliberately kept separate from NWH's tactical recommendations below, and they don't guarantee any particular expansion speed for the alliance.
💡 General recommendation from the strategy guide (not from the resistance table below): to generally take part in fighting Mutant Beasts, a virus resistance of roughly 2,500 or more and a squad power of roughly 25 million or more are recommended. This is an approximate, general figure that doesn't guarantee success against a Level-1 dig site specifically — the table below shows that a Level-1 site itself requires resistance of about 4,000, higher than the general recommendation.

🧮 How does virus resistance apply within a Rally?

The rally leader's virus resistance applies to every participant, except anyone whose own resistance is higher than the leader's — that member keeps their personal value.

Example: Leader = 2,500 — Member A = 3,000 — Members B, C, D = 1,000 each.

Resistance actually applied in combat: Leader = 2,500 — A = 3,000 (keeps their own value since it's higher) — B, C, D = 2,500 each (raised to the leader's level).

So it's best for rallies against dig sites to be led by members with high virus resistance — this lets the rest of the participants benefit from that resistance and reach faster, safer occupations.

Unit matchups against monsters: Gorillas (aircraft) are weak against missile units, Bears (missile) are weak against tanks, Mammoths (tank) are weak against aircraft. Alliances with tank-heavy power are advised to target Polar Bears first.

⚠️ 12. Common Mistakes
  • 🪨 Poor coal management: spending coal randomly instead of saving it for factory and furnace upgrades — significantly slows your progress during Week 1.
  • 🐌 Late development: delaying the High-Heat Furnace or first titanium factory — every day of delay pushes back the rest of the chained unlocks.
  • 🔥 Unused furnace: owning a personal furnace without running it regularly — misses mission goals and exposes you to freeze risk.
  • 🧊 No heating before a storm: not preparing before a blizzard arrives leads to freezing and blocks movement and rallies.
  • 🤝 Poor member coordination: not gathering near the Alliance Furnace leaves some members outside the collective warming range.
  • 🧱 Disorganized base placement: not following the 3×3 + two-empty-tile layout makes collective defense harder and leaves gaps for the enemy.
  • ⚔️ Unprepared attacks: trying to occupy a dig site or city without enough virus resistance or enough rallies leads to unnecessary losses.
  • 🔀 Confusing individual and collective events: such as mixing up Purge Action (individual) with Purge the Polluted Area (alliance) — leads to wrong expectations about rewards and ranking.
