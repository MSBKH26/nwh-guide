⚔️ VS Day 2 – Base Expansion

How to Reach 3.6M and 7.2M Easily

📢 Introduction

Hello everyone,

Tomorrow we start VS Day 2 – Base Expansion, and we continue with the same idea we started on Day 1: better organization, proper resource saving, and using each resource on the day when it gives the most points.

To make things clear, here is a simple example showing that reaching 3.6M on Day 2 does not require a huge amount of resources.

🎯 How to Reach 3.6M Easily
4 UR Trucks800,000 pts
8 UR Secret Tasks1,200,000 pts
200 Survivor Recruitment Tickets600,000 pts
6 days of Construction Speedups1,080,000 pts
TOTAL3,680,000 pts ✅
With these resources alone, you already pass 3.6M and unlock the required reward box — and this calculation does not even include the points earned from increasing Building Power.

The idea is simple: you don't need a huge amount of resources. You need to save the right resources and use them on the right day.

🚀 Can You Reach 7.2M? Absolutely!

If you have already reached 3.68M and still have resources available, there is no reason to stop. Let's add to the previous example:

150K Building Power increase3,375,000 pts
3 additional days of Construction Speedups540,000 pts
Additional points3,915,000 pts
3,680,000 + 3,915,000 = 7,595,000 pts 🎉
That means you can pass 7.2M and unlock all VS reward boxes on Day 2!
📌 Important Note

These calculations are only examples. You do not need to follow them exactly. You may have more or fewer Speedups, buildings ready to complete, more Survivor Recruitment Tickets, or other opportunities to score points.

The purpose of these examples is simply to show that 3.6M is not difficult when you prepare for the day properly, and that 7.2M is also a very realistic target for anyone who has enough resources and uses them wisely.

🏆 Our Day 2 Target Is Clear
3.6M — Minimum
7.2M — Optimal Target

If you have the resources to continue, do not stop at 3.6M. Push toward 7.2M and unlock all your VS reward boxes.

Plan your construction, use your Speedups wisely, and don't forget your UR Trucks, UR Secret Tasks, and Survivor Recruitment Tickets.

The right resource on the right day = more points, more rewards, and more power for both you and the alliance. 💪🔥
